Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

CEVYQ Group ("CEVYQ", "we", "us") makes digital business cards and related pages for businesses. This page explains what information we collect, why, how long we keep it, who can see it, and how you can ask us to remove it.

Who this applies to

  • People whose digital card we build (our clients)
  • People we contact to offer a card (leads: business owners, agents, tradespeople)
  • Visitors to our cards and websites (preview.cevyq.com, cevyq.com, and client card pages)

What we collect and why

From leads (people we contact about a card):

  • Name, business name, email address, and sometimes a phone number or WhatsApp number
  • Where it came from (for example a public business directory or property listing site)
  • Used only to send you one outreach message and, if you reply, to follow up about building your card

From clients (people who buy a card):

  • Business name, logo, services, contact details, and photos you give us to put on the card
  • Payment confirmation (we do not store card or bank numbers ourselves; payment goes through Wise or PayPal, who hold that information under their own policies)
  • Used to build, host, and update your card, and to send you your free monthly report if you are a paying client

From visitors to a card or website:

  • Basic visit information when a card page is opened: roughly when, on what kind of device, and general location (country/city, not exact address), so the card owner can see that their card was viewed
  • We do not ask visitors to log in and do not collect visitor names unless they fill in a form or booking widget on the card, in which case we collect what they type (for example name and number) so the card owner can get back to them
  • A visitor's own device may offer a "day picker" or booking tool. Information typed there goes to the card owner and to us only to show the booking on the card owner's dashboard

How long we keep it

  • Lead contact details: up to 90 days from when we first contact you, then deleted, unless you reply or ask us to keep in touch
  • Client details and card content: kept for as long as the card is active, plus a reasonable period after in case you want it reactivated; deleted on request
  • Visit/open information: kept so the card owner can see activity history; not linked to a visitor's real identity beyond what they typed into a form themselves

Who sees it

  • CEVYQ (Chris) can see all of the above
  • Sales agents who help find leads can only see that a message was sent and whether it led to a sale, for their commission tracking. Agents do not see client card content, visitor data, or any dashboard information belonging to a client
  • We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone else
  • We do not share your details with any other business unless you ask us to, or the law requires it

Where it's kept

  • Card pages and client website code: GitHub (private code storage) and Cloudflare (for paying clients) or GitHub Pages (for demos and testing)
  • Our dashboard and visit statistics: Cloudflare Workers and a Cloudflare database (D1)
  • Lead lists we collect before contacting people: Dropbox, access limited to CEVYQ
  • Email: standard email providers (Gmail/business email)

Your choices

  • Leads: every message we send includes a way to ask us to stop contacting you (reply "unsubscribe" or similar, or use the link/instruction in the message). We will remove your details within a reasonable time and not contact you again
  • Clients: you can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the information we hold about your card
  • Visitors: if you filled in a form or booking widget on a card and want that information removed, contact the card owner directly, or contact us and we will pass on the request

We will stop contacting you and action a removal request within 5 business days of receiving it, and will fully answer any other request within 14 days.

Where you are protected

This policy is written with these laws in mind:

  • United States: the CAN-SPAM Act, including honest sender identification, a working way to opt out of further email, and honoring opt-out requests promptly
  • South Africa: the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), including collecting only what is needed, keeping it secure, giving you the right to object to direct marketing, and letting you access or ask for deletion of your information
  • Australia: the Privacy Act 1988 and the Spam Act 2003, including identifying ourselves clearly in commercial messages and providing a working unsubscribe option that we act on quickly

Security

We take reasonable steps to keep information secure, including private code storage, password-protected dashboards, and not sharing data beyond what is described above. No system is 100% secure, but we do not collect more than we need and we delete information that is no longer needed.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
