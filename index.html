Questions

Straight answers.

Do I pay every month for a card?+

No. A digital business card is one payment, with no monthly fees. If you want changes later, we tell you the small fixed price first.

Can I see it before I pay?+

Yes. We build it with your logo and details and show you a preview of the finished thing. The buttons and links switch on once it's paid.

How fast is it?+

Cards are usually ready in a day. For landing pages and software we agree a date with you before any work starts.

What do landing pages, software and lead lists cost?+

Every business is different, so we ask a few quick questions on WhatsApp and then give you a fixed price before any work starts. No surprises.

Where do the leads come from?+

Public business listings for your trade and area. A person then checks every lead, and dead numbers and closed businesses are removed before you get the list.

Which countries do you work in?+

The UK, the US, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa and Thailand. Prices are shown in your own currency.

Who will I be dealing with?+

Chris, the founder. One person on WhatsApp from the first message to the finished job.
